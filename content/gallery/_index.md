# Gallery
> If you hear a voice within you say 'you cannot paint,' then by all means paint, and that voice will be silenced.
{quotee="Vincent van Gogh" link="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_van_Gogh"}


I have been drawing for as long as I can remember---from doodles in boring classes to sleepless nights spent finishing a portrait. Over time, my art style has varied, ranging from colorful brushstrokes to intricate stipples. I have failed to preserve a lot of drawings, leading to stains, or tears.
